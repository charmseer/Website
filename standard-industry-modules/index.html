The Product

What It Is

VR training, opened up

AutoVRse builds VR training for industrial workforces. This app opens a set of those modules to anyone with a Meta Quest, so teams can try them on their own headsets.

Each module is a real procedure rebuilt in VR, not a summary or a walkthrough video. The equipment behaves the way it does on the floor, the steps follow an actual work instruction, and you learn by doing. The modules run on the same engine as AutoVRse's enterprise deployments.

Built for first-timers

  • Hand tracking or controllers, whichever the learner prefers.
  • Voice guidance and visual prompts at every step.
  • Getting it wrong is safe: the module corrects you and carries on.
  • Most sessions run 5 to 15 minutes.

Training you can measure

Every full module comes with the Pulse Dashboard. Instead of a simple pass or fail, step-level tracking shows exactly where a learner hesitated or struggled.

Pulse IDs run independently of the headset's Meta account, so even shared headsets give clean, per-person data, with manager and learner views and LMS integration.

Who it's for

  • Teams that already have Meta Quest headsets and need content for them.
  • Safety leads who need standardised, repeatable training now, without waiting months for a custom build.
  • VR studios and systems integrators adding credible industrial content for their clients.
  • Buyers who want proof before committing to a custom deployment.

How it's offered

The app is free to download. The short modules, including PPE Fundamentals and LOTO Basics: Mechanical Safety, are free to run. Full modules unlock per headset, with the complete procedure, an assessment and Pulse tracking.

The Modules

Twelve Procedures

LOTO: Mechanical Free

Lock out and tag out mechanical equipment safely before maintenance.

PPE Fundamentals Free

Select, inspect, fit and use the right protective equipment for the job.

LOTO: Electrical

De-energise and lock out electrical systems, from isolation to verification.

Electrical Safety

Handle live electrical systems and equipment safely, with correct isolation.

Gas Cutting

Set up and operate oxy-fuel cutting equipment, without real heat or fire risk.

Welding Safety

Spot hazards and follow safe practice during arc welding.

Conveyor Belt

Diagnose and replace a flat belt on a sorting line, minding pinch points.

Working at Height

Use harnesses and fall protection correctly for elevated work.

Confined Spaces

Run entry checks and controls, and respond to emergencies.

Fire Safety

Recognise fire hazards, use an extinguisher and evacuate correctly.

Forklift Safety

Operate a forklift and handle loads safely around people on the floor.

Defensive Driving

Build hazard perception for on-road and industrial site vehicles.
