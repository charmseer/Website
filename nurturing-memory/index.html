The Game

About Nurturing Memory

The story

Visit your grandmother after years of being away. Choose to rebuild your relationship with her, navigate shifting roles of care, and tend to her garden.

The act of gardening works as a metaphor for caregiving, and the state of the garden shifts in response to her physical and mental struggles. Balance aspects of caretaking and challenges of the day to day with your mental health.

Features

  • Gardening through mini-games, management, and strategy.
  • Dialogue-driven storytelling explores family relationships.
  • Garden morphs to reflect grandmother’s health.
  • Exploration to unlock dialogue and grandmother’s memories.

My Role

Lead Unity Developer

I lead development on a four-person core team. When I joined, the project had only its initial scene setup. I built the game's playable prototype from there, and I'm now taking it from that prototype to a full game.

Prototype to alpha

Built the first playable version of the game, and grew it into the alpha build the team playtests every few months.

Technical direction

Planned how the game grows from a short prototype into a full game, so the team can keep adding story and content without rebuilding what works.

Gameplay

Built the game's core systems, from gardening and the passing of days to weather, an in-game phone, inventory, memories and saving.

Story

Brought the writer's dialogue into the game with Yarn Spinner, so the story plays out as the designer intended.

Tools & testing

Built editor and playtest tools for the team, backed by automated tests and fixes from each playtest round.

Team workflow

Run version control and builds for the team, including a separate branch for art, and turn the Game Director's feedback into playable builds.
